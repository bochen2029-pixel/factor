# QC · Reviewer A · Internal consistency and correctness of BLUEPRINT v0.1

Lens: the spec on its own terms, checked against the three law documents it claims to implement (`ORG-SOLVER_THE-IRREDUCIBLE-OPERATIONS`, `ORG-SOLVER_RECONCILIATION_FIVE-VS-EIGHT`, `THE-TOOL_DEPUTY_THE-SEAT-THAT-ACTS`, all 2026-09-08). Severity 1: the spec contradicts itself or a stated law, or a gate as written cannot pass. Severity 2: two implementers would build different systems from the same text. Severity 3: terminology drift, missing denominators, wording. Evidence for the table rows (falsifier matrix, gate walk, state machine, byte arithmetic) is in the appendices after the keep-list.

## What is right and must not change

- The seam law and its placement: the pass proposes, the seam disposes, nothing learned disposes; margins are logit differences read on a fork, verbs come from a closed set, DO is the last branch of the gate.
- Record separate from Effect: holds are on the tape with their margin and reason, and the ledger holds only the standing verb.
- Allocation as a pass over the whole set with overflow to HOLD, never to acting; keep it, and give it a law (finding 10).
- The monotone license law with the ratification row as the console's image of the yes, the kernel able to raise the stratum and never lower it, expiry so nothing is permanent, and the audit floor that never reaches zero.
- Per-verb horizons, both-sides calibration before a band is licensed, and the canary as a ratified reversible-only lottery inside the take-back window: the ideas are the reconciliation doc's corrections carried faithfully; only the wiring (findings 1, 18, 20, 24) is broken.
- The pin pair minted together, asserted at boot, checked at restore; a changed weight without a pin refuses to load.
- The physical two-process split with a module gate rather than a policy; keep it and extend it to the process tree (finding 7).
- References, not copies: the tape holds judgments and receipts, the spools hold the world, frames are referenced by (lane, offset, hash), and `verify` needs no model.
- Fixed-point amounts, walks sorted by id, thresholds in the writ, and a decode failure that is fatal rather than a stale margin.
- Idempotency keyed by (id, judged_rev) and the `judged_rev` field itself, which the reconciliation doc asked for.
- Every falsifier ships with a planted lie.
- The writ readable and never writable by the kernel, the switch as a file, and the enumerated powers the model does not have (§20).
- A 128-byte hot record with no strings, DONE as a transient until the horizon, and the dossier as the only home for text.
- The organ contract: exit codes branched on, `--about`, inert output, degraded answers with code 6 instead of crashes.
- "Judgment may be delayed; the world is never edited": the ring blocks the producer, the spool is the backlog, the mark is taken before the model loads.

## Appendix C · State machine as written

Entries: OPEN (obligation row); HELD (HOLD); WAITING (WAIT); DRAFTED (DRAFT); STAGED (DO, windowed); DONE (STAGED released; also DO inverse-carrying, implied); ASKED (ASK); PASSED (PASS); CLOSED (discharge predicate, from any state).

Exits: OPEN, HELD, WAITING, DRAFTED are re-gated. STAGED → DONE on release; no exit on reversal. DONE → CLOSED at the horizon; no exit on a bad outcome. ASKED → nothing defined (sign, decline, later, horizon all unspecified). PASSED → nothing; not walked, not compacted. CLOSED → compaction; no reopen.

Events with no state effect: UNSAID, REVERSED, EXPIRED, DEMOTED, ROLLED-BACK, GRADUATED, COMPILED, GROWN. The fold that produces the ledger (§13) does not read `asked`, `answered`, `draft`, `unsaid`, or `rollback` rows. See findings 4, 14, 31.
