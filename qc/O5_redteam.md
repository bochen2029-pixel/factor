# O5 · ADVERSARIAL QC of FACTOR BLUEPRINT v0.1

Lens: how §§1, 9, 10, 11, 15, 20 fail against a capable attacker or ordinary misuse; how §§10, 13 can be gamed or drift.
Severity 1 = money or data leaves, or authority is taken, or a stated law is enforced by nothing. 2 = drift, gaming, denial of attention, or a misuse an ordinary operator will hit. 3 = hygiene that becomes a hole later.

Nothing below asks for a new subsystem the design does not already imply. Every fix is one or two sentences of spec text.

## Laws that hold as written

These survive an adversarial reading and should not be traded away in fixing the above.

1. **§1.3 — every verb recorded with its margin; silence is on the record with its number.** The design's strongest idea. It converts the failure that leaves no trace — the decision by omission — into a row, and it is what makes every other audit possible.
2. **§1.4 — one writer, one valve, the inverse or the window carried with the effect, recorded before it leaves.** The ordering is right: record-then-act cannot produce an act with no row, and the reverse ordering can. Nothing in the attack surface breaks it.
3. **§9 step 8 — "Running out of people degrades to holding, never to acting."** The correct default under load, and F-BUDGET tests it with the right planted lie. Preserve this exactly; R27 refines who gets the scarce attention, not what happens when it runs out.
4. **§9's separation of pressure from threshold — "a loaded queue changes what is looked at first, not how much evidence an act needs."** This is the single best sentence in the document. It defuses the entire urgency-lowers-the-bar family of manipulations, which is how most social-engineering against a human assistant works.
5. **§10's monotone direction — narrow on evidence alone, widen only within a covering ratification, and the reducer may never author the rule.** The direction is right and F-MONOTONE tests it. R9, R10 and R22 attack the *authentication* and the *durability* of that law, never its shape.
6. **§10 F-BOTH-SIDES — a band is not calibrated until both sides of the threshold clear the floor.** Blocks the classic drift where a system licenses itself on the acts it took while its wrongful holds stay invisible. Rare to see stated at all.
7. **§6's deterministic span location — "a candidate whose span does not locate is refused," with F-EXTRACT's planted lie being offsets emitted by the model.** Exactly right: the model may not report its own evidence coordinates. This is the pattern every other check in the system should copy.
8. **§12 — references, not copies; the tape holds judgments, the spools hold the world; `factor verify` needs no model.** A verifier with no model in it is the correct trust anchor, and separating the deputy's record from the world's record is what makes replay meaningful.
9. **§5's identity and revision discipline for structured sources** — id from source coordinates, idempotent replay, lower revisions refused and counted, an order-independent digest proving a replay is bit-identical. Holds fully for structured lanes; R23 is about the prose lanes it does not cover.
10. **§19's determinism laws** — fixed-point amounts, walks sorted by id, thresholds pinned, two runs byte-identical, and *"a decode failure is fatal with a row, never a stale margin."* That last clause is the right fail-closed instinct; R8 generalizes it to the rest of the gate.
11. **§16's exit-code contract — branch on codes, never on text — and `--about` with pins.** Correct as an interface law. R7 is a failure of the *gate* to act on the codes, not of the contract.
12. **§18's pin pair** — serve-bytes hash and weights hash minted together, both asserted at boot and checked at restore, a changed weight without a new pin refusing to load, and no lineage self-promoting. Sound against model substitution, provided the pins live behind R9's signature.
13. **§1.6's four-rung shape with an audit floor that never reaches zero, and expiry as the default.** "Nothing is permanent because it was once approved" is the right stance; R30 fixes how expiry lands, not that it exists.
14. **§0 and F-CONSERVE — never drop a percept, with an exhaustive five-way partition every frame must land in.** An exhaustive partition is the right shape for a conservation law; it makes silent loss a test failure rather than a hope.
15. **§21's methodology — every falsifier ships a planted lie that must fail.** "An oracle that only passes measures nothing" is correct and rare. Several individual lies are too weak (R5, R13, R19 name them); the discipline itself is the asset.
